To:     Negotiating Committee, The Zinnia

From:   Compensation Intern

Re:     Local Hotel Wage Survey

For your upcoming negotiations with Local H-56, you requested
that I undertake a local wage survey.  I have computed average
wage rates from a sample of local hotels.  These are the
preliminary results:

        Baker                $24.36
        Bartender            $18.49
        Bellstand            (applicable state or local minimum wage)
        Broiler Cook         $24.13
        Cashier              $17.31
        Dishwasher           $16.93
        Host                 $19.73
        Hostess              $19.23
        Housekeeper          $17.30
        Skilled Cook         $26.61

I am currently working on trying to compare the benefit packages,
but this is quite complicated.  A cursory investigation shows that
The Zinnia's benefit package is comparable to other union hotels in
the Twin Cities, but quite generous relative to the nonunion ones.
For example, no nonunion hotel I spoke with offers fully-paid health
insurance, not even for single coverage. Also, no nonunion hotels have
a leave of absence policy beyond the minimum paid sick leave required
by the City of Minneapolis Sick and Safe Time ordinance or the State
of Minnesota Earned Sick and Safe Time (ESST) law.
