The Zinnia

Minneapolis, MN 55555

Dear Hotelier:

According to our records, your union agreement with Local H-56 is expiring shortly. I want to remind you that other contracts will be negotiated next year and a large settlement at The Zinnia can be expected to have significant ramifications on those negotiations. More importantly, the convention business is very competitive and I want to make sure you realize that any increase in rates will have an adverse impact on our ability to attract conventions to the Twin Cities. The same is true for our ability to secure agreements to host major sporting events like the Super Bowl or the NCAA Final Four.

Additionally, if the Twin Cities hotel industry develops a reputation for having labor problems, our attractiveness to travelers and to the organizations that book conventions and sporting events will suffer.

At the same time, to attract conventions and visitors, Minneapolis needs to improve its image to be more strongly seen as an inclusive city welcoming of all guests, and commited to the improvement of racial justice for its residents. There are many things that your organization can do on its own to support these efforts. I encourage you to look at the Racial Equity Dividends Index created locally by the Center for Economic Inclusion as just one starting point for ideas. Moreover, hopefully you can find some ways to involve the union in these efforts. At a minimum, please ensure that your employees are respectful of all guests, which might involve training or other interventions.

Sincerely,

Director
