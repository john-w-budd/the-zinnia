Contradictions in the modern age? No. The Zinnia service for the business traveler. Service as polished as the marble flooring in the lobby. Amenities as plush as the over-sized bath towels in every guest room.

The Zinnia prides itself on outstanding service and amenities. Each guest room features complimentary wireless high-speed secure internet access, plenty of bedside outlets, a full-size desk, and a high-definition flat-panel TV. You will also find plush over-sized terry towels and robes, real drinkware to accompany your complimentary in-room coffee and snacks, luxury toiletries, and fine chocolates on one of your many pillow choices. Suites and a penthouse with fireplace, grand piano, and a fabulous view of downtown Minneapolis, round out the luxurious options. The Zinnia offers a full business center, concierge services, valet parking, overnight laundry and dry cleaning, 24-hour room service, and 24-hour airport transportation.
Over 10,000 square feet of meeting space is perfect for business and social events of all sizes. From board meetings to banquets, we will work closely with you to deliver the perfect event. Boardrooms all contain the latest in high-definition presentation technology and fast, secure internet access. The Zinnia's expert staff serves your guests with the same luxurious service provided overnight guests.
The Zinnia Grille features distinguished service and classic American cuisine in a luxurious setting. The Zinnia Lounge offers a full-service bar and another intimate setting in which to relax, conduct business, or celebrate.

The Zinnia is located in the heart of the Minneapolis business district. Enclosed skyways connect The Zinnia to numerous business and social destinations. Whether you are traveling, hosting a meeting, or just looking to get away, come to The Zinnia and experience Service from the Golden Age, Connectivity for the Digital Age.
