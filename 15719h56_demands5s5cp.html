Local H-56: Internal Memos
(access restricted to the Local H-56 bargaining team)
To:Local H-56 Negotiating Committee for The Zinnia
From:The Union Stewards
Re:Rank and File Demands
 
We conducted an informal survey of the members of the bargaining unit at The Zinnia over the previous month. The following is a list, in no particular order, of rank and file demands they would like to see won in the upcoming negotiations.
Wage Increases: 5% per year, no lump sums.
Wage improvements for the jobs tied to the minimum wage. Our pay is stagnating!
Health Insurance: AFFORDABLE HEALTH INSURANCE IS A MUST! Dependent coverage at no cost; Lower prescription drug co-pays; Eye care
Paid sick leave. No more delays--compliance with Minnesota's Earned Sick and Safe Time (ESST) mandate NOW!
No paycheck deductions for the Minnesota Paid Leave program. The Zinnia should pay the full premium (that is, pay 100% of the payroll tax).
Vacations: Two weeks after one year of service, three weeks after four, additional week for each additional four years of service
Holidays: Day after Thanksgiving, Martin Luther King's Birthday, Juneteenth, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Yom Kippur, Rosh Hashanah, Diwali, Christmas Eve, double time for holidays worked
Work Schedules and Safety: Seven day posting period before beginning of new schedule. Safer workloads. Harassment protections.
Panic buttons for room cleaners and room service staff. No room cleaning apps. Extra time to clean rooms that have skipped daily cleaning because those rooms are a lot dirtier and have more laundry to lug around.
Overwork: Double time for time worked on the employee's seventh day of work. Bring back our staffing levels to at least pre-pandemic levels.
Day Care Benefits: YES!
Dental Insurance: YES!
Subcontracting: None allowed.
Real progress on social justice, not just lip service. Protections for immigrant workers. Ban ICE from the workplace. Translators for meetings with management.
