Additionally, the employer contribution to the health and welfare plans
in some major cities exceeds $4.00 per hour. Contracts in Boston, Las Vegas,
and elsewhere also require employer contributions for education or training
funds, housing funds, and legal funds to help workers in those areas. In
some cases these contributions are 15¢ per hour or higher, but even a
few cents per hour can be a good starting point for a new innovation.

In some locations, the pension fund contribution is over $2.00 per hour.
The administration of 401(k) plans cost $4.00 per employee per month.

Good Luck in negotiations.

Fraternally Yours,


Director, Research Department
